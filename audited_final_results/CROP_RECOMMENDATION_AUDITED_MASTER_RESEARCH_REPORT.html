Scientific Limitations & Future Scope

  • Continuous Distance Memorization Bounds: While zero exact duplicates and healthy nearest-neighbor distance ratios (> 1.0) were confirmed, nearest-neighbor distance in 7-dimensional continuous space is sensitive to feature scaling and density distribution. Future work should incorporate topological data analysis (persistent homology).
  • Synthetic Augmentation Trade-off for Random Forest: Augmenting Random Forest with massive synthetic datasets (165,000 samples) reduced holdout accuracy from 99.32% to 90.91% due to boundary blurring in axis-aligned decision trees. In contrast, XGBoost (99.77%) and TabNet (99.09%) proved highly robust to synthetic augmentation.
  • Agronomic Rule Verification in External Fields: The stress scenarios simulate environmental shocks via mathematical transformations. Future agronomic research should validate these recommendations using empirical multi-year multi-location field trial datasets (ICAR/FAO agricultural registries).
